# India Factor Investing & Strategy Rotation Analytics (2006–2026)

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents a historical analysis of factor-based investment strategies across the Indian equity market from 2006 to 2026.

The objective is to understand how different investment strategies perform across various market cycles, identify annual performance leaders and laggards, and evaluate the consistency of different factors over the long term.

The analysis covers nine investment strategies:

- Alpha
- Beta
- Growth
- Momentum
- Opportunities
- Value
- Multifactor
- Quality
- Low Volatility

Using historical market data and Power BI, this project examines annual strategy performance, factor rotation patterns, long-term price trends, and rolling investment returns.

The project aims to answer an important investment question:

**Does one investment strategy consistently outperform the market, or does leadership rotate across different market cycles?**

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

1. Analyze the historical performance of nine investment strategies from 2006 to 2026.
2. Identify the best-performing and worst-performing strategies in each calendar year.
3. Understand how factor leadership changes across different market cycles.
4. Compare the historical performance of different investment strategies.
5. Evaluate 1-year, 3-year, and 5-year investment returns.
6. Identify the consistency and volatility of different investment approaches.
7. Build an interactive Power BI dashboard for investment research and decision-making.

The ranking visualization covers 2006–2026 and highlights:

## 🔍 Key Research Questions

This analysis explores several important questions:

- Which investment strategy has delivered the strongest historical performance?
- Does Momentum continue to outperform across different market environments?
- How does Value investing perform during market recoveries?
- Does Low Volatility provide relatively stable performance during market corrections?
- How frequently does the leading strategy change?
- Can a strategy that underperformed in one market cycle become a top performer in the next?
- Is long-term performance more important than short-term performance when selecting an investment strategy?

## 💡 Investment Research Perspective

One of the central themes of this project is that investment strategy leadership can change over time.

A strategy that performs exceptionally well in one year may underperform in another. Therefore, selecting an investment strategy solely based on its recent performance may not provide a complete picture.

Historical analysis across multiple market cycles can help investors understand:

- Performance consistency.
- Strategy rotation.
- Risk and return characteristics.
- Long-term investment behaviour.
- The importance of diversification.

The analysis is intended as a historical research and educational resource, not as a recommendation to invest in any particular strategy.

---

## ⚠️ Important Notes & Limitations

- Historical returns do not guarantee future performance.
- Annual rankings represent historical observations and should not be interpreted as future predictions.
- 2026 performance may represent a partial calendar year.
- Investment conclusions should consider risk, drawdowns, transaction costs, taxes, and other relevant factors.
- The methodology, data sources, return treatment, and rebalancing assumptions should be considered before interpreting the results as an investable strategy.
